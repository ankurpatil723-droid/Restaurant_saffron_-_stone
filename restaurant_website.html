Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dining, online delivery, dietary needs, and payments.

Do you offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free choices? +
Yes, absolutely! Our kitchen prepares clearly marked vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes. Each menu card lists allergens such as dairy, gluten, or nuts, and our staff is always delighted to tailor preparations for vegans.
How does online ordering and delivery work? +
Select dishes into your basket, choose Delivery or Takeaway, apply coupon WELCOME10, and proceed. Your order summary generates instantly for WhatsApp checkout, and you can pay conveniently via UPI QR code or upon delivery!
Can I make reservations for large groups or private celebrations? +
Yes! For parties of 8 or more, we recommend using the reservation form or connecting directly on WhatsApp so our culinary team can arrange dedicated seating and custom tasting menus.
What are your delivery fees and minimum order limits? +
Our minimum delivery order is ₹250. Standard delivery fee is ₹49, and delivery is completely free for orders of ₹500 and above! Takeaway and dine-in carry zero delivery fees.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept all UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, net banking, and cash upon delivery or dine-in.
