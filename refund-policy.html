Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Because all meals at Saffron & Stone are freshly prepared to order using perishable ingredients, we maintain clear guidelines regarding order cancellations and refunds.

Important Notice: As stated during checkout, UPI payments are verified manually via WhatsApp screenshot / UTR confirmation. In case of payment issues or cancellations, please contact our support desk immediately via WhatsApp.

1. Order Cancellation Policy

  • Before Preparation: You may cancel your order within 5 minutes of placing it by messaging our WhatsApp support desk (+91 72639 90838). A 100% refund will be issued.
  • After Kitchen Commencement: Once our chef has begun preparation (after 5 minutes), orders cannot be cancelled or refunded as the food cannot be reused.
  • Delivery in Progress: Orders already dispatched with our delivery courier cannot be cancelled.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

You are eligible for a replacement or a full/partial refund in the following circumstances:

  • Incorrect Items: If the items delivered do not match your confirmed order receipt.
  • Missing Items: If any paid item was missing from your delivery parcel.
  • Quality/Damage Concerns: In the rare event of food spillage or verified preparation defects reported within 30 minutes of receipt with a photo.
  • Delivery Delays: If our delivery exceeds 75 minutes beyond the promised delivery window due to our operational delay.

3. Refund Processing & Mode

  • Approved refunds are processed back to the original source account (UPI ID or bank account) within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • Alternatively, you may opt for instant store credit or a replacement meal sent with priority dispatch.

4. Table Reservations Cancellation

  • Table reservations are completely free to book and modify.
  • If your plans change, please notify us at least 1 hour in advance via WhatsApp so we can release the table to waiting guests.

5. Contact Our Support Desk
