Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Saffron & Stone ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when browsing our website, placing dining orders, booking tables, or subscribing to our newsletters.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to prepare your artisanal meals, arrange table seating, and process payments:

  • Order Details: Full name, delivery address, 10-digit phone number, dietary notes, and ordered items.
  • Table Reservations: Name, email address, phone number, party size, reservation date, time slot, and special requests.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Email address provided voluntarily for seasonal tasting invites.
  • Payment Data: UPI transaction reference IDs or UTR numbers you share with us. We never store credit/debit card numbers or bank passwords on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your food orders accurately.
  • To coordinate reservations with our front-of-house host desk via WhatsApp.
  • To communicate order status, delivery dispatch, and payment receipts.
  • To prevent fraud, verify UPI transfers, and ensure server security.
  • To send occasional seasonal menu announcements (only if subscribed).

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is stored securely in our encrypted Supabase database protected with Row Level Security (RLS) and environment-level secrets. We only share information with:

  • Direct delivery partners solely for completing your food delivery.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) when you voluntarily trigger order or reservation confirmations via direct chat.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website utilizes local storage on your device only to preserve your shopping basket items across refreshes so you do not lose your selections. We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.
